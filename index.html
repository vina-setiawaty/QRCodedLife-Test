QRCodedLife

Digital companion pieces for the QRCodedLife exhibition series. Each prototype below is a self-contained experience that extends a physical installation about QR codes.

Prototype 1 The Obelisk Navigate a 3D model of the exhibition obelisk. Each face carries four QR codes — tap one to open its story. Prototype 2 Obelisk — modal links Same obelisk; only 3 QR codes are active. Tapping one opens a modal and updates the URL (e.g. …/obelisk-modal/modal-1/), so the modal itself can be linked to directly. Prototype 3 Obelisk — separate pages Same 3 active QR codes, but each one navigates to its own standalone page (e.g. …/obelisk-pages/page-1/) instead of opening a modal.
